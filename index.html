06 / QUESTIONS

Short answers.

Is AI Spend my bill?

No. It’s your usage priced at public API rates. Missing estimates are left out, never counted as $0.

What leaves my Mac?

Midas connects directly to your providers to read usage. It does not upload your prompts or conversation text. Optional Sync shares numeric usage through your own private hub or folder; login credentials are never synced.

Which providers?

Guided setup covers Codex, Claude, Cursor, and Meta, including separate personal and work accounts. 50+ more live in Settings → Providers.

How is DGX Spark valued?

Recorded local tokens are compared with a named hosted API rate. It’s an API-equivalent estimate, not a bill or a savings claim; unknown usage stays unpriced.

Which Macs?

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Can I rename my accounts?

Yes. Open Settings → Providers, choose Codex or another saved-account provider, then edit Account labels. Names save automatically on this Mac. Reset restores the default label; account connections and usage history stay unchanged.

How do updates work?

Signed, notarized, and installed in-app. On 0.36.0 or earlier? Download once; updates are automatic after that.
