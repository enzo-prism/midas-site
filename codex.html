04 / QUESTIONS

Codex usage,
answered.

How do I check my Codex usage limits on a Mac?

Midas keeps your Codex 5-hour and weekly limits in the Mac menu bar, each with the percentage left and its reset time, so you can check them without opening ChatGPT settings.

What are Codex reset credits in Midas?

Midas headlines how many Codex reset credits an account holds, for example “2 available”, and adds “1 usable now” when OpenAI reports credits that apply to your current limits. Expiry dates appear in account details.

Why does a credit show as available but not usable now?

OpenAI reports zero applicable credits whenever no limit currently needs a reset. Midas keeps both numbers, so credits you hold never disappear from view.

Can I track several Codex accounts?

Yes. Connect each OpenAI account separately from the Codex CLI login, label them, and pick which one drives the menu-bar ring. Each account’s quota, credits, and history stay with that account.

Which Codex limit drives the menu-bar ring?

The weekly limit of the account you choose, or its monthly allowance when there is no weekly window. The 5-hour session limit never drives the ring.

Is the Codex spend estimate my bill?

No. Midas prices the tokens in your Codex sessions at public API rates to show what that usage would cost through the API. It is an estimate, not a charge on your ChatGPT plan.
