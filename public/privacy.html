Website privacy

Updated October 27, 2026. This notice covers midas-ai.dev, operated by Prism.

Your choice

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” “No thanks” prevents it from loading. Your choice is saved in this browser for 180 days. Use “Analytics preferences” in the homepage footer to change or withdraw consent. Withdrawing clears this site’s Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page with the tag disabled.

Optional Google Analytics

When enabled, Google receives information about website visits and interactions: page URLs and titles, referral and campaign information, browser/device information, approximate location derived from your connection, and events such as outbound links, scrolling, and download clicks. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish visits. Our tag limits cookie lifetime to 180 days and does not refresh it on each visit. Ad personalization and Google signals are disabled. Email redaction and redaction of common sensitive query parameters are enabled; please do not put private information in website URLs.

We use this information to understand how people discover Midas and whether they click a download link. A download click does not prove installation or app use. Event-level retention uses GA4’s two-month setting; aggregated reports may remain available longer. Google’s privacy policy and Google’s Analytics opt-out tool provide more information and controls.

Cookieless website analytics

We also use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate page visits. It does not use analytics cookies. See Vercel’s analytics privacy documentation.

The Mac app

Website analytics does not receive Midas account labels, provider credentials, prompts, conversations, or tracked AI usage. App provider connections and optional private usage sync are separate from this website. See the app setup guide.

Contact

For website privacy questions, contact Prism. Avoid including credentials or private account information in public issues.
