04 / QUESTIONS

Gemini usage,
answered.

How do I track my Gemini usage on a Mac?

Install Midas 0.51.0 or later and choose Gemini. Midas shows your Gemini quota in the Mac menu bar, pinned to the Google account you choose, with reset times for each window.

Does Midas show Gemini 5-hour and weekly limits?

Yes, when the source reports rolling windows, as the Antigravity path does. The Gemini CLI path reports Pro and Flash quota windows instead, and Midas shows those.

Which Google account does Midas use for Gemini?

The one you pin in Settings → Providers → Gemini. Pinning keeps Midas from silently reading a different Google login.

How is Gemini spend estimated?

Midas prices the tokens in your local Antigravity transcripts at Google’s public Gemini API rates. Gemini CLI token usage is not part of the estimate yet, and unknown models stay unpriced.

Does Midas read my Google cookies?

No. Gemini in Midas uses OAuth sign-ins and local sources only, never browser cookies.
