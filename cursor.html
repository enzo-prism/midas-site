04 / QUESTIONS

Cursor usage,
answered.

How do I see my Cursor usage limit on a Mac?

Midas shows your Cursor allowance in the Mac menu bar with the percentage left and its reset time. On unified plans that is the shared weekly pool; on legacy plans it is your monthly Cursor Models and Other Models pools.

What is Cursor’s shared weekly pool?

On Cursor’s unified plans, introduced October 12, 2026, Cursor, Grok Bot, and Bugbot draw from one weekly pool. Midas shows it as the headline limit with its weekly reset.

How does Midas calculate Cursor spend?

On unified plans, Midas reads Cursor’s daily usage feed, which reports tokens and billed cost per product, and values plan-included usage at the current week’s rate per token, so earlier days are approximate. Legacy plans use Cursor’s per-request usage events and reported costs.

Does Midas need a Cursor API key?

No. Midas uses your existing cursor.com browser sign-in, cached in your Keychain. A manual Cookie header is available for advanced setups.

What happens when Cursor can’t be reached?

Midas keeps the last good spend reading and labels it last known, rather than showing $0.
